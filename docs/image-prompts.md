# Portfolio image prompts

These four prompts define the original visual language used across Adeoye Precious's portfolio.
They can be reused to extend the illustration set without borrowing brand assets from the visual
reference.

## Hero data story

An editorial marker-and-crayon illustration on warm cream paper. Show a confident Black woman data
analyst at the centre of a playful data story, surrounded by hand-drawn charts, grids, arrows, dots,
and small analytical symbols. Use cobalt blue, vivid orange, grassy green, warm yellow, charcoal,
and a small amount of pink. Loose human linework, rich marker texture, premium contemporary
editorial composition, generous negative space, no words, no logos, no interface screenshot.

## HR attrition

An editorial marker illustration on warm cream paper about understanding why people leave a
workplace. Show a row of varied abstract people, one moving through an open doorway, connected to a
small bar chart and branching analytical marks. Cobalt blue, orange, green, yellow, charcoal, and
pink accents. Friendly, thoughtful, tactile, hand drawn, no words, no logos.

## Ride-hailing analysis

An editorial marker illustration on warm cream paper about trip patterns in a city. Show a playful
map path, location pins, a compact car, connected dots, and a rising chart woven into one coherent
scene. Cobalt blue, orange, green, yellow, charcoal, tactile crayon texture, no words, no logos.

## Weather and MovieLens studies

Create two related warm-paper editorial illustrations. One combines clouds, rain, sun, temperature
marks, and a simple prediction line. The other combines film frames, stars, viewing dots, and a
recommendation network. Keep both expressive, analytical, human, and richly textured in cobalt,
orange, green, yellow, charcoal, and a restrained pink accent. No words, no logos.
